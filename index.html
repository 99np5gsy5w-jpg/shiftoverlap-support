Support

A hand with your schedules.

How do I set up a rotating pattern?

Open Schedules, add a person and choose the first date of cycle day 1. Use a starter pattern or tap each cycle day to set work hours. Add days until the whole rotation is represented, then save. Repeat for the second person. Patterns extend before and after the first cycle date.

How do night shifts and sleep work?

A shift ending at or before its start time ends the next day. Equal start and end times mean a 24-hour shift. Daily sleep applies every day; recovery adds time after each shift. Turn daily sleep off if it does not fit rotating nights, and use recovery to protect post-shift sleep.

Why does a day off still show unavailable time?

A day-off exception removes the shift starting on that date. A night shift from the previous day, its recovery, daily sleep and personal plans can still block time.

Why do I see no shared windows?

Check both patterns, their first dates, sleep and recovery settings, and exceptions. Reduce Minimum shared window in Settings if shorter gaps are useful. The next-overlap card searches 60 days ahead and excludes elapsed time.

What happens when clocks change or I travel?

All times follow your selected household time zone. Rotations advance by local calendar dates. Missing spring clock times move to the next valid time; repeated autumn end times use the later occurrence. Durations use elapsed time. Changing the household zone keeps your entered dates and clock times.

What is included in the subscription?

ShiftOverlap Monthly unlocks your shared-time results, calendar and image export. You can set up and read your schedules without active access. Eligible Apple Accounts receive one week free, then US $1.99 per month. Apple displays local prices and eligibility before purchase. See subscription details.

How do I restore or cancel?

Use Settings → Trial & subscription → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe. To cancel, use Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ShiftOverlap. Deleting the app or resetting data does not cancel billing.

Where is my data?

Schedules are stored on your device and may be included in its backup. We do not have a copy. Use Reset saved data in Settings to remove the active schedules. Exported images are snapshots and cannot be recalled. Read the privacy policy.
