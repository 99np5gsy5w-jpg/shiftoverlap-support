Importing an event or schedule link

In the build 78 beta, you can enter any public HTTPS event or schedule address in Link. Kinly checks which uses the publisher permits. An event can still carry a link when automatic reading is unavailable.

When Kinly can read a source

Choose a source and confirm that you own the material or have permission for the use shown. Tap Find events when you are ready. Kinly can read an event page and use OpenAI when the recorded permission covers both. For a calendar feed approved for structured import, Kinly reads the calendar fields without AI. Review the proposed events before saving to Family or Private Space.

Confirmation applies to the selected source and calendar space. Choosing a different source or additional schedule pages requires confirmation for that selection. Access to Private Space and permission to process source material are separate. An AI import can send the selected material to OpenAI outside your restricted calendar share. Kinly privacy information

When automatic reading is unavailable

Use Open original site to view the schedule in your browser, or Enter event with this link to enter an event yourself and keep the address with it. You can also choose a calendar export, a supported file or text you have permission to import. Kinly uses no AI credits to offer these options or keep a reference link. Opening the original site sends an ordinary browser request to that site.

For example, a link to a school or team schedule with no recorded permission can still be attached to a manually entered event. If the organizer offers an export, check the rights for the file and your intended calendar use. This applies to pasted text, screenshots and attachments too.

Each grant covers specific addresses and uses. Kinly checks links reached by redirects and any additional pages. A publisher can restrict access even when permission has been recorded.

Publishers and organizers

Any organizer or platform can ask Kinly to review a source. Provide a public feed or page address and public terms covering automated retrieval, calendar use and any permitted AI processing. Include attribution, geographic limits, expiry and the right to share saved events with household members, where applicable. For a removal or correction request, identify the relevant public source. Keep private invitations, personal documents, credentials and purchase records out of public issues. Kinly support
