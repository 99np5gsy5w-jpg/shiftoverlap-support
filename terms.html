ShiftOverlap Monthly

More time together.

One week free for eligible Apple Accounts, then US $1.99 per month.

Apple displays the price in your local currency and introductory-offer eligibility before you confirm. Prices may differ by country or region. Trial access begins when you confirm with Apple.

Included with active access

Shared-time calculations for two entered schedules, the next free window, the shared-time calendar and calendar image export. You can set up and read saved schedules without an active subscription. Example mode uses fictional schedules.

Renewal and cancellation

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or current billing period. Apple may charge for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

Restore purchases

In the app, open Settings → Trial & subscription → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that subscribed. Restoring does not create a new subscription.

Limits of the schedule

Results depend on the schedules and personal plans you enter. The app does not know about employer changes, appointments or other calendars unless you add them. Both people use one household time zone. Exported images are snapshots and do not update.
